Problem Statement & Strategic Opportunity

Identified Enterprise Pain Points
CV–Reality Gap (Bad Hire Risk)
Recruiters spend 45 min/candidate with zero evidence of real-world skills. CV readability ≠ job performance. High bad-hire rate inflates recruitment costs significantly.
Fragmented Recruitment Operations
Data scattered across spreadsheets, Google Forms, email threads. Zero single source of truth for cross-functional hiring decisions. Coordination overhead is extreme.
Invisible Skill Gaps Post-Hire
New hires overwhelmed by unstructured onboarding. L&D managers lack real-time skill matrices to plan training ROI. Promotion paths remain vague and unmeasured.

Part B · Workflow 2 — New Hire Onboarding

New Hire Onboarding — Closed-Loop Feedback Swimlane

Process Design Insight
Closed-Loop Architecture: When Line Manager marks evidence as "Fail", the system triggers feedback and automatically cycles the New Hire back to revision. Loop continues until evidence meets benchmark — no substandard output passes through.
4 Process Actors
HR Ops — Configure & initiate onboarding flow
System — Auto-notify, track & certify to HRIS
New Hire — Do Sim, submit evidence, revise on feedback
Line Manager — Review evidence, Pass/Fail with notes

Part B · Workflow 3 — Upskilling Continuous Loop

Upskilling — Continuous Learning Closed-Loop

Design Philosophy
Perpetual Growth Engine: The loop never terminates. When promoted, the system reloads the next competency framework at a higher benchmark — creating an evidence-based, automated career progression ladder.
Loop Logic
L&D Manager → Define benchmark competency framework
AI Engine → Detect gap, assign sim, score, check benchmark
Employee → Complete upskill simulation, submit result
Pass: Skill Matrix updated → Promotion Signal triggered
Fail: Remediation sim assigned → Loop restarts
